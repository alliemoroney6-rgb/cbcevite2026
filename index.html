About Catholic Brothers for Christ

We are a group of men, of various ages and places in life, who are deeply committed to having a relationship with Jesus Christ and sharing the Gospel through the witness of lively brotherhood rooted in prayer, the Sacraments, mentorship, and service.

Learn More About Us

Our Mission

To inspire and empower men to fully embrace Christ's purpose for their lives through wholehearted prayer, authentic brotherhood, and intentional acts of service.

Our Vision

A brotherhood of spirited and courageous missionary disciples, united in faith, striving to cultivate a Christ-centered culture throughout North Texas.
